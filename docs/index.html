Mounting and processing settings

Boat

A 6-axis IMU has no compass, so slow heading drift and boat turns are removed with the window above. To get true angles, zero the oar: hold it perpendicular to the boat, tap the gunwale 3 times and hold still until the LED lights solid. The zero is applied from that moment until the next one (and back to the start of the file for the first). Without it, sweep is relative to the average oar position. Catch and finish are told apart automatically: the drive is the half of the stroke where the blade is lower.

Gyro bias is re-estimated whenever the oar is still for a second, so hold it still for a few seconds after switching on.

Files: copy ROWxxxx.CSV from the SD card and open it, or drop it anywhere on this page. Columns t_ms, ax_mg, ay_mg, az_mg, gx_ddps, gy_ddps, gz_ddps; other CSVs with t, ax…gz columns in g and °/s also load.
